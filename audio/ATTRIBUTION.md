# Japanese speech audio

The 100 WAV files are synthesized locally from this project's original practice text using Open JTalk / pyopenjtalk-plus and the **HTS Voice Mei (mei_normal)**.

Voice model license: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). This use is not endorsed by the voice creators. The original notice is included in LICENSE_mei_normal.txt.

Software: [pyopenjtalk-plus](https://github.com/tsukumijima/pyopenjtalk-plus) (MIT), Open JTalk (Modified BSD). Installed build tools retain their license notices in .tools/python. Build tools are not loaded by the webpage.

Changes: the voice model was used to synthesize new practice sentences; slow playback changes the playback rate without changing pitch. No personal data or answer history is part of the audio.

manifest.json records waveform duration, amplitude and hashes. texts.json records the exact synthesis input. Speech is synthetic, not a native-speaker recording; accent and naturalness have not received independent human review.
